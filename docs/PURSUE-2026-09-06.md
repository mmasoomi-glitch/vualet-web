## What changed

### 1. `npm test` exists now — `86c1a96`

There was no `test` script at all. 31 test files on disk; only 19 were
reachable by any script. **Twelve were reachable by none**, including the
accessibility, support-route, tenancy-adversarial and payment-lifecycle
suites. Now: `node --test "scripts/*-test.mjs"` → **898 tests, 0 failures**.

The glob is quoted so *node* expands it, not the shell. An unexpanded glob
runs zero tests and still exits 0, which is worse than having no script.

> **Trap for the pod.** This suite FAILS on the sophia pod, and those failures
> are not real. `scripts/revenue-counterfactual-test.mjs` extracts old
> revisions with `git show 4ed719e / f889ee5 / 1ade97d`; the pod's clone has
> no such history. A pod job had already started "fixing" them — it was
> stopped, because the fix would have been to weaken working security tests.

### 2. The support widget stopped telling customers it refunded them — `1d41467`

`POST /api/support` answered anyone who typed "refund":

> *"Refunds are available within 14 days of a charge — I've logged your
> request, and you'll see it back on your original payment method within 5–10
> business days."*

The route has no database, no mail transport and no payment call. Nothing was
logged. It also offered to "pull your latest invoice, update your payment
method" (it cannot) and pointed at "Settings → Billing" (not a place in this
app). `site-chrome.tsx` mounts it on **every public corporate page**.

Now a signpost that claims nothing and names only real destinations. Guarded
by `scripts/support-promises-test.mjs`, which asserts the *property* rather
than the wording: no reply, on any of 11 inputs, may claim a completed or
automatic action, or name a path the app does not serve. **Counterfactual
run** per `gotchas#138/#139`: restore HEAD's route and the guard exits 1.

### 3. Marketing stopped claiming more than the terms claim — `9f6fac7`

`/legal/terms` §2 says Mira is *"**designed** not to fabricate or invent…a
deliberate **design commitment**"*. The marketing asserted it as settled fact
in eight places, including the figure **`0`** over *"invented facts, ever"*.

Replaced with the claim that **is** evidenced — a grounding check runs on
every reply and rewrites an unsupported answer into an honest "I don't know"
before it is sent (`verifications#908`, 8/8 adversarial cases). Memory claims
scoped to the customer's own group, which is also the truth.

14 insertions, 14 deletions. Headline structure, tone, layout and every other
tile untouched — this is a compliance alignment, not a brand rewrite, and the
**wording is open to owner re-taste at deploy review**.

### 4. `/mira/live` — `2be717e`

Fulfils SENATE `req_687be77117c9`. The draft it replaces carried an unmeasured
metrics strip ("under 180ms", "99.7% uptime target", ∞ over "memory recall"),
a simulated console, and transcripts of Mira booking hospital transport and
starting navigation. **Zero numbers on the page now**, future tense
throughout, stamped *"Being built · no date"*.

Its CTA said *"Join the waitlist"* and pointed at `/mira/start`. **There is no
Live Voice waitlist** — `/mira/start` is the real onboarding wizard, and
`/signup` is the corporate private-alpha list for a *different* product which
redirects Mira visitors away. The button now says what it does.

### 5. Two buttons that said "Notify me" and notified nobody — `34d9fdb`

`/mira/store` ScreenWatcher and Locksmith cards. No href, no handler, no
endpoint. Now describe state instead of inviting a dead click.

## What was checked and is FINE — do not "fix" these

- **All 84 interactive controls have accessible names.** `gotchas#463` (a
  suite reporting 3/3 passing while seven controls had none) is closed. I did
  not trust the green report — I wrote an independent sweep and had to fix it
  twice before its number was trustworthy (locating the end of a JSX opening
  tag with the first `>` is wrong; `onChange={(e) => …}` contains one).
  *Scope limit:* programmatic **names** only. Contrast, focus order and
  keyboard traversal still need a browser against a running host.
- **"Messages are encrypted at rest on our servers" is TRUE.**
  `message-log.mjs:38` encrypts before the INSERT, via a per-tenant DEK
  envelope in `packages/state/src/crypto.mjs:183`.
- **The VPN preview page is adequately hedged**, and *"nothing is sent to our
  servers"* describes the preview widget — which really is client-side only.

An auditor that cannot see the system reports UNVERIFIABLE, and unverifiable
is not false. Both security claims it could not check turned out to be true.
